- Changes: Embedded textures resized to maximum 1024 pixels; opaque diffuse/base-color maps converted to JPEG quality 88; normal, packed metallic/roughness, emission, and transparency maps retained as PNG. Original geometry, PBR material maps, hierarchy, and animations retained.
- Files: archimedes.glb, dalton.glb, hippocrates.glb, pythagoras.glb, watson.glb.

Original source describes five spaceships made for Naev with diffuse, emission, metallic, roughness, and normal textures. Metallic and roughness channels remain packed in the original glTF material map layout. See source.json for original and optimized SHA-256 checksums and model statistics.
