# Starflight quality assets

## PBR spacecraft

Archimedes, Dalton, Hippocrates, Pythagoras, Watson by Viktor Hahn. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/proteron-ships . Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Textures resized/repacked for web delivery; authored geometry/materials retained. See ships/source.json and ships/attribution.md for original and final hashes.

## Scanned environment and terrain

Poly Haven Boulder 01, Coastal Cliff 02, Fir Sapling, Jacaranda Tree, Sand 01, Aerial Rocks 01, Aerial Grass Rock, Snow 02. CC0-1.0 https://polyhaven.com/license . Geometry simplified and textures resized for this game. Source URLs, authors and exact final file hashes: environment.json. The internal key palm is a broad-canopy Jacaranda model, not a botanical palm.

## Architecture

City Kit Commercial2.1 by Kenney https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial , CC0-1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Skyscrapers A/C/E and Building I; embedded original palette texture in GLB. No procedural placeholder boxes.

External assets retain their original CC0/CC BY4.0 licenses. The Studio DK project license does not override these licenses. No paid marketplace assets or extracted viewer-only resources are included.
